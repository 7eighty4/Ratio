================================================================================
                    RATIO — LEGAL THINKING & SPEAKING GYM
                             Project Description
================================================================================

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ratio is an interactive, web-based legal training platform designed specifically
for Indian law students, moot court competitors, judicial service aspirants, and
aspiring trial/appellate advocates. 

Named after the foundational legal concept "Ratio Decidendi" (the legal rationale
or principles behind a judicial decision), Ratio provides a structured, high-intensity
workout environment to develop three critical skills:
  1. Rapid Legal Concept Recall
  2. Time-Bound Legal Research & Analysis
  3. Succinct, High-Pressure Oral Advocacy & Synthesis

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In traditional legal education, students often spend hours reading lengthy case briefs
and commentaries, but rarely get the opportunity to practice spontaneous, timed oral
advocacy. 

When faced with unexpected judicial queries on the bench, moot court interruptions, or
client consultations, many advocates struggle to condense complex legal principles into
clear, 60-second oral summaries.

Ratio addresses this gap by creating an "on-demand legal gym" where users cannot predict
the legal topic drawn, forcing them to:
  - Quickly retrieve legal knowledge under pressure.
  - Locate relevant Bare Act sections, precedents, or theoretical principles within a 10-minute research window.
  - Articulate a cogent, persuasive legal explanation in exactly 60 seconds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW RATIO IS USEFUL FOR USERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Moot Court Preparation:
   - Simulates surprise bench questioning and sharpens impromptu oral submissions.

B. Judicial Services & Bar Examination Preparation:
   - Reinforces memory of core constitutional doctrines, statutory sections, landmark Supreme Court cases, legal maxims, and jurisprudence.

C. Courtroom Advocacy Skills:
   - Teaches advocates how to state the core "Ratio" of a case without rambling, using precise legal terminology.

D. Self-Assessment & Voice Recording:
   - Users can record their speech, listen back to analyze their tone, pace, and clarity, and read automated speech-to-text transcripts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. KEY FEATURES & FUNCTIONALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• 3D Slot Machine Topic Reel:
  When a user clicks "DRAW TOPIC", a 3-second slot-machine drum reel animates, 
  decelerating smoothly and locking onto a random legal topic with audio feedback.

• Comprehensive 150+ Legal Topic Library Across 17 Practice Categories:
  1. Constitutional Law (Basic Structure, Article 21, Article 32 Writs, Federalism, Article 44 UCC)
  2. Jurisprudence & Legal Theory (Positivism, Natural Law, Hart-Fuller Debate, Legal Realism)
  3. Landmark Indian & Global Cases (Kesavananda Bharati, Maneka Gandhi, Puttaswamy, Donoghue v. Stevenson)
  4. Statutes & Legislation (BNS vs. IPC, RTI Act 2005, IBC 2016, DPDP Act 2023, IT Act, PMLA)
  5. Contract Law (Frustration Sec 56, Promissory Estoppel, Minor Agreements, Smart Contracts)
  6. Law of Torts (Strict vs. Absolute Liability, Vicarious Liability, Defamation, Medical Negligence)
  7. Criminal Law & Criminology (Culpable Homicide vs. Murder, Insanity Defense, Death Penalty)
  8. Property Law (Lis Pendens, Rule Against Perpetuity, Part Performance, Mortgages)
  9. Administrative Law (Delegated Legislation, Legitimate Expectation, Wednesbury Test)
  10. International Law (UNCLOS, R2P, Diplomatic Immunity, ICJ Jurisdiction)
  11. Human Rights Law (Non-Refoulement, UDHR, Custodial Violence & DK Basu Guidelines)
  12. Legal Ethics & Responsibility (Contempt of Court, Advocate Duties, Free Legal Aid Art 39A)
  13. Judicial Institutions & Bench Dynamics (Collegium vs. NJAC, PIL Evolution, Recusal)
  14. Legal Maxims (Audi Alteram Partem, Nemo Judex, Res Ipsa Loquitur, Actus Reus/Mens Rea)
  15. Logical Fallacies in Advocacy (Straw Man, Ad Hominem, Slippery Slope, Red Herring)
  16. Legal History & Evolution (Magna Carta, Code of Hammurabi, Constituent Assembly Debates)
  17. All Categories Practice Mode.

• Retracting Circular Progress Timer:
  Visual SVG circle outline that retracts counter-clockwise as time counts down. 
  Features a circular settings button to configure research durations (3m, 5m, 10m, 15m, 20m, or custom).

• 60-Second Speech Studio with Waveform & Transcription:
  Real-time audio waveform visualization, microphone recording, and automatic 
  speech-to-text transcription powered by Web Speech API.

• Local IndexedDB History Log:
  Sessions are saved locally in the browser's IndexedDB database (`ratio_db`). Users can 
  replay past voice recordings, read transcripts, check date/time stamps, or purge records.

• Custom Glassmorphic Modal System:
  In-app custom React dialogs for post-session saving and session deletion confirmations.

• Default Dark Theme:
  Modern dark aesthetic (`#080C0A`) with glowing emerald accents (`#10B981`) and Garamond serif typography.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Framework: React 18 with TypeScript
- Build Tool: Vite
- Styling: Tailwind CSS & Custom CSS Variables (Glassmorphism design system)
- Database: IndexedDB via `idb` wrapper
- Audio Synthesis & Ticking: Web Audio API (Oscillators & Gain Nodes)
- Speech Recognition: Web Speech API (`webkitSpeechRecognition`)
- Audio Recording: MediaRecorder API
